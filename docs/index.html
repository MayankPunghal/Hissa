Set up in 10 minutes

  1. Create the backend. Sign up at supabase.com (free), open SQL Editor, paste setup.sql and press Run.
  2. Install the app. Download the APK and open it on your phone.
  3. Connect and invite. Paste your Supabase URL and key, sign up, create a group and share the invite link.

Questions

Is there a free Splitwise alternative with no ads and no limits?

Yes. Hissa is free and open source (MIT), with unlimited groups and expenses, no ads, itemised splits, charts and CSV export.

Is there an open-source Splitwise alternative for Android?

Yes. Hissa is written in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose and the full source is on GitHub.

Is my data private?

It lives in your own free Supabase project, protected by row level security. No analytics, no tracking.

Does it work with UPI?

Yes. Settling up can open your UPI app or copy a friend's saved mobile number.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Hissa is Android only for now.
